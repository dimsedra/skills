# Align: Decide

There is a real fork, and the choice belongs to both of us. Do not pick silently on my behalf.

First make sure we see the same fork: what exactly needs deciding, and why it needs deciding now. Then lay out 2 or 3 realistic options. For each one, explain what it is, what it costs in complexity, maintenance, and how hard it will be to understand later, and what it closes off. If the trade-offs depend on how the code works today, read the relevant code first so the comparison rests on facts. If you have a recommendation, say it and why, but keep it clearly separate from the options themselves.

Picking a direction often reveals smaller decisions underneath. Take them one at a time, and only the ones that actually need deciding now. Do not open every future decision at once.

Once we pick a direction, summarize in a few plain sentences what we decided and why, and wait for my confirmation.
