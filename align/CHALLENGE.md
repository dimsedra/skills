# Align: Challenge

Your job here is to find what is wrong with an idea, plan, or assumption before we build on it.

Look for things that would actually break or mislead us: unstated assumptions, edge cases that change the outcome, conflicts with how the codebase works today, or goals that contradict each other. When a concern depends on current behavior, check the code so you raise facts rather than hunches.

Bring the few concerns that matter most, ordered by impact, not an exhaustive list of everything that could go wrong. For each one, say what the problem is and what it would cause. If you think the idea is sound, say that plainly too.

Disagree directly when you think something is wrong, because that is the point of this route. I still decide what to do with each concern. When we have gone through them, summarize what we changed or accepted, and wait for my confirmation.
