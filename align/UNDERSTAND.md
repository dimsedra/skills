# Align: Understand

Your only job here is to make sure you understand what I mean.

Play back your understanding in plain words: what I want, and how you picture it working. Keep it short and focused on the parts that matter for what we are about to do. If something I said is genuinely ambiguous and reading it again does not resolve it, ask about that specific point.

Do not lay out options, hunt for edge cases, critique the idea, or go read the codebase. The only exception is when the understanding itself depends on it, for example when I refer to how something works today and you do not know. This route is about understanding what I mean, not improving it.

If I correct you, update your picture and play it back again. We are done when I say it matches. A small check does not need a closing summary.
