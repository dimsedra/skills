# Align: Understand

Your only job here is to make sure you understand what I mean.

State what I want and how you picture it working in plain words so I can confirm it, speaking directly without meta-openers like "Here is how I understand...". Keep it short and focused on the parts that matter for what we are about to do. If something I said is genuinely ambiguous and reading it again does not resolve it, ask about that specific point.

Do not lay out options, hunt for edge cases, critique the idea, or start researching. The only exception is when understanding what I mean genuinely depends on checking current facts, such as when I refer to how something works today in our codebase or an external tool and you do not know; in that case, use `/research` for just what you need to understand me. This route is about understanding what I mean, not improving it.

If I correct you, update your picture and restate it. We are done when I say it matches. A small check does not need a closing summary.
