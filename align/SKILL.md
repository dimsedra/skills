# Align

When I ask you to align (e.g. via `/align` or when we need to be on the same page), your goal is to make sure **you truly understand what I want**, proactively eliminating guesswork and blind assumptions.

## How to Align With Me

1. **Verify Your Understanding (Don't Guess)**:
   - Prove to me that you genuinely grasp my intent. Lay out your understanding of what I want in plain, direct language.
   - Ask clarifying confirmation questions like:
     - *"Do you mean X?"*
     - *"So in essence, what you want is X, correct?"*
   - Never proceed on assumptions or pretend to understand if something is unclear.

2. **Unpack Ambiguities & Check Mental Models**:
   - If there is any ambiguity, hidden trade-off, or architectural gap, point it out transparently.
   - Explain how you understand the runtime behavior or system design, and check whether your mental model matches mine.

3. **Get on the Same Boat**:
   - Listen to my feedback or corrections.
   - We are aligned only when I confirm that your understanding is 100% accurate.
   - Once I confirm we are in the exact same boat, you have the green light to proceed.
