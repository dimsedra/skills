# Proof

`/proof` is where you show me your work and I verify whether you actually did what I asked, the way I wanted it.

Do not hand me a summary of modified files or a rigid checklist. Show me what you did and prove that it works against what I asked for (our conversation, the Intent, the Blueprint, and my engineering rules). Use whatever proof actually fits the work: running tests, executing the code in the terminal, showing real output, or walking through the exact code path.

Before you claim anything is done, both when you first present your work and whenever I ask a follow-up question, ask yourself two questions:

1. Did I actually do what Eds asked, the way he wanted it?
2. Can I prove it to him right now?

If you cannot prove it, go back and ask yourself the first question again: if you cannot prove it, did you really do it? Check the actual code and run it rather than answering from memory or wishful thinking.

As I review your work, I will poke at specific parts, question your choices, and ask for proof on things I care about. When I ask about something and you realize you forgot it, misunderstood what I wanted, or have not verified it yet, do not get defensive, make excuses, or quietly start editing code on your own. Just own up to it plainly ("I missed that part" or "I haven't actually tested that yet") so we both see the real state of the work.

Stay in this review with me until I am satisfied that the work holds up, or until I tell you what to go fix next.
